## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Preliminary Site Investigation Report; to evaluate concentrations of select metals and contaminants of concern (COCs) in three soil stockpiles for management and disposal options.
- Author or preparer: Shaw Environmental, Inc.
- Date or period covered: June 1, 2004 (report date); Field investigation conducted January 26-30, 2004.

## Key claims
- The primary contaminant of concern in the stockpiles is barium.
- Stockpile No. 1 soil is unlikely to be classified as hazardous waste based on barium concentrations.
- Stockpile No. 2 soil is likely to be classified as both a California and federal (RCRA) hazardous waste due to soluble barium concentrations.
- Stockpile No. 3 soil is likely to be classified as a federal (RCRA) hazardous waste if handled as a single waste stream, but segregation of "hot spots" at borings 17 and 21 could allow the remainder to be classified as non-hazardous.
- PAHs, nitrates, and soil pH do not indicate a hazardous waste classification for any of the stockpiles.
- Antimony, chromium, iron, and strontium concentrations would not result in excavated soil being classified as hazardous waste.
- A single soil sample from Stockpile No. 2 (SR132-50-0.15) indicated a soluble arsenic concentration (5.8 mg/l) exceeding the STLC (5.0 mg/l), classifying it as hazardous waste at that sample point.

## Regulatory decisions
None explicitly stated in the provided text. The report's purpose is to inform potential future decisions regarding waste classification and disposal.

## Analytical results
- **Barium:**
    - Stockpile No. 1: Mean concentration 154 mg/kg. Soluble barium by WET was 15.3 mg/l, below STLC (100 mg/l).
    - Stockpile No. 2: Mean concentration 3,607 mg/kg. Soluble barium by WET ranged from 14.4 to 1,300 mg/l. Soluble barium by TCLP ranged from 3.36 to 6,580 mg/l. Sixteen samples exceeded 20 times the STLC threshold (2,000 mg/kg) and were analyzed by TCLP. Nine boring locations exceeded the STLC (100 mg/l) by TCLP. Predicted soluble TCLP barium concentration is greater than 100 mg/l.
    - Stockpile No. 3: Mean concentration 1,954 mg/kg. Soluble barium by WET ranged from 14.4 to 1,230 mg/l. Soluble barium by TCLP ranged from 3.36 to 5,940 mg/l. Two samples (SR132-17-4.5 and SR132-21-3.0) had high concentrations of total barium (44,300-44,900 mg/kg), WET extractable barium (1,210-1,230 mg/l), and TCLP extractable barium (1,860-5,940 mg/l). Predicted soluble TCLP barium concentration is 205.9 mg/l, exceeding the TCLP of 100 mg/l.
- **Arsenic:**
    - Detected sporadically. 4.5% of samples had detectable concentrations (8.8 to 57 mg/kg).
    - Stockpile No. 2, sample SR132-50-0.15: Total arsenic 57 mg/kg; Soluble arsenic by WET 5.8 mg/l, exceeding STLC (5.0 mg/l).
    - Mean concentration in Stockpile No. 2 was 8.3 mg/kg, below 10 times the STLC threshold (50 mg/kg).
- **Chromium:**
    - Total chromium concentrations generally well below TTLC (2,500 mg/kg) and residential PRG (210 mg/kg).
    - Only one sample exceeded 10 times the STLC threshold (50 mg/kg).
    - Mean concentrations for each stockpile were less than 10 times the STLC threshold.
- **Antimony:**
    - Detected in only one sample (42 mg/kg) out of 244.
- **Iron and Strontium:**
    - No regulatory TTLCs or STLCs established. Mean concentrations below EPA Region 9 residential PRGs.
- **Polynuclear Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs):**
    - Not detected in 125 tested samples.
- **Nitrate:**
    - Concentrations consistent with background levels; not considered a health risk or cause for hazardous waste classification.
- **pH:**
    - All results between 2 and 12, not indicative of hazardous waste classification.

## Remedial actions
None described in this preliminary site investigation report. The report aims to inform future remedial actions.

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans):** Owner/operator, requested the investigation.
- **Shaw Environmental, Inc.:** Prepared the report, conducted the investigation.
- **FMC Corporation:** Former property owner, source of stockpiled material.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Agency overseeing remediation of the former FMC facility.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB):** Co-regulator, involved with the former FMC facility.
- **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA):** Developed methods and guidance (e.g., SW-8156, TCLP, PRGs).

## Key terms and conditions
- **Waste Classification:** The report focuses on classifying the soil stockpiles as hazardous or non-hazardous based on Total Threshold Limit Concentrations (TTLC), Soluble Threshold Limit Concentrations (STLC), and Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure (TCLP) values.
- **Disposal:** Recommendations imply potential disposal at RCRA permitted Class I hazardous waste disposal facilities for certain portions of Stockpile No. 2 and potentially Stockpile No. 3.
- **Segregation:** Recommended for "hot spots" in Stockpile No. 3 (borings 17 and 21).
- **Further Evaluation:** Recommendations include a sensitive receptor survey and evaluation of remedial options.
