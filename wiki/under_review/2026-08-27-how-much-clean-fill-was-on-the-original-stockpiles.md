Based on the wiki articles provided, there is no specific data on the total volume of "clean fill" that was on the original stockpiles since the 1960s. The concept of "clean fill" is only discussed in relation to **post-remediation capping and grading activities** that occurred after 2020. Here is the relevant information:

- **Stockpile 1** was capped with DTSC-approved clean fill material sourced from the Carpenter Road excavation area, Basin 5, and the Carpenter Road shoofly by fall 2020. The original stockpile itself was **34,000 cubic yards** of barium-containing soil, not clean fill. [[Stockpile 1#Capping, erosion, and remediation]]
- **Stockpile 2** required a minimum 12-inch clean fill cap, sourced from the Carpenter Road underpass excavation area and sorted MSE wall footing spoils. The original stockpile was **102,000 cubic yards** of barium-containing soil. [[Stockpile 2#Clean cap sourcing]] [[Stockpile 2#Origin and characterization]]
- **Stockpile 3** was completely excavated and removed to other containment zones; no clean fill cap was applied to its original footprint. [[Stockpile 3#2020 complete removal (SR-132 West Project)]]

**Conclusion:** The wiki does not document any clean fill volume on the original stockpiles from the 1960s. Clean fill was only imported after 2020 as a capping material.
