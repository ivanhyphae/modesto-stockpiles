Based on the corrected wiki data, here are the answers:

- **Yes, the December 2023 thallium exceedance was also present at the background location.**
  - Concentrations from Table 2 of the storm water reports: SW-East 18 µg/L, SW-West 15 µg/L, and **BG-West (background) 17 µg/L** [[thallium]].
  - All three results sit within ~20% of the detection floor against a 15 µg/L laboratory reporting limit [[thallium]].

- **No, thallium is not a contaminant of concern at this site.**
  - The background location exceeded alongside the site locations, which rules out a site release [[thallium]].
  - A stockpile release cannot elevate the background sample to the level of the site samples [[thallium]].
  - This is considered a single anomalous sampling day at a reporting limit poorly matched to its own standard (2.0 µg/L MCL) [[thallium]].
  - Thallium was non-detect in every subsequent event (Feb 2024, Feb 2025, Feb 2026) [[thallium]].
  - DTSC approved each report with no thallium comment or follow-up requirement [[thallium]].
